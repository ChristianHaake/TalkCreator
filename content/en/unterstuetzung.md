# Support TalkCreator

TalkCreator is free to use. Contributions are voluntary and do not unlock extra features.

## What contributions support

Contributions support TalkCreator's maintenance, bug fixes, and testing. Talk plans stay in the browser and are not sent to an app server.

## Contribute

[Support TalkCreator through Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/Haake)

This link opens an external provider. TalkCreator sends no data to Buy Me a Coffee before you open the link. The provider's [privacy policy](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy) applies after you leave the app.

## Help without paying

- [Report a bug](https://haak3.atlassian.net/jira/software/form/69a50efc-0474-445e-8f01-66eb6b747cc1)
- [Suggest a feature](https://haak3.atlassian.net/jira/software/form/50d4fc51-bff4-4e21-b46e-8fd987b90d24)
- Alternatively, email [apps@haak3.de](mailto:apps@haak3.de).

Include your browser and reproduction steps, but do not share personal conversation content or project exports.
