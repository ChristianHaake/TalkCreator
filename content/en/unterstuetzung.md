# Support TalkCreator

TalkCreator is free to use. Contributions are voluntary and do not unlock extra features.

## What contributions support

Contributions support TalkCreator maintenance, bug fixes, and testing. You can also help by reporting bugs or sharing the app.

## Support TalkCreator

[Support TalkCreator through Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/Haake)

No data is sent to Buy Me a Coffee while you view this page. The link goes to an external provider. After you open the link, [Buy Me a Coffee’s privacy policy](https://buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy) applies.

## Feedback

[Report a bug or suggest a feature](mailto:apps@haak3.de?subject=TalkCreator%3A%20Feedback). For bug reports, include your browser and reproduction steps, but do not share personal conversation content or project exports.
